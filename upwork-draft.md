## 4. Rates & Service Packages

### Suggested hourly rate

**Start at [$110–$125/hr]. Once you have Upwork reviews, move up to [$150–$175/hr].**

Why this range:
- Upwork's own guide puts "Advanced / Expert" freelancers at $60–$120+/hr. It says highly specialized consulting roles "often reach $75–$150+ per hour." [1]
- One Upwork proposal guide says established cybersecurity consultants usually charge $75–$150/hr, and specialized work runs $150–$250+/hr. The same guide warns that brand-new profiles with high rates need strong evidence to back them up. It also recommends fixed-price first phases. [2]
- Upwork's penetration tester cost page puts experts at $120–$150+/hr. It's a different specialty but a useful senior benchmark. [3]
- Upwork's Cybersecurity Experts listing shows profile rates from about $10 to $200/hr, with senior profiles commonly at $100–$200/hr. [4]
- An outside US contract-rate benchmark lists cloud security architects at a $100/hr median, rising to about $163/hr at lead/principal level. [5]
- **Caveat:** Budgets in Upwork job *posts* run much lower. One tracker of 499 recent Information Security & Compliance posts found a $35/hr median (top 10% at $60+) [6], and a "Senior Cloud Security Consultant" post offered $40–$65/hr [7]. At principal level, look for enterprise or regulated-industry clients, and don't compete on price with low-budget posts.
