Four questions that decide the next twelve months:

1. Who is the principal?
Not “the bot.” A named identity, short life, only the tools that job requires.

2. What did it invoke?
If you cannot replay the tool calls later without guessing, you cannot do IR and you cannot do audit.

3. Where does “stop” live?
If stop lives in the prompt, it is a suggestion. Stop has to live in the control plane and survive retries.

4. Who can revoke the session in under a minute?
If the answer is “we’ll retrain the prompt,” you already lost the incident.
