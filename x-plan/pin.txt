Two things I keep seeing people get wrong:

1. Lights in the sky. Most of what people call “craft” is Starlink, Iridium, aircraft, or a known military bird. Hold your phone up. Heavens-Above, Find Starlink, FlightRadar24. If it has a catalog number, it isn’t a mystery.

2. AI agents. The model is not the incident. The incident is an agent that can leave the sandbox, hit a 200, and keep going with no named principal, no short-lived creds, and no kill path outside the prompt.
