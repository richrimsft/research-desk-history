## BODY

Tesla promised the new Roadster for 2020. It's October 2026 and nobody has one. Tonight at 8:55pm ET, at a SpaceX rocket test site in McGregor, Texas, Elon says we're about to watch "a candidate for greatest product demo of all time." I'm watching. I also want a delivery date.
Source: Elon Musk on X: https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2110549767063507044
Source: Reuters (via Yahoo Finance), event time and location: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tesla-set-unveil-next-generation-100401819.html

### What they promised in 2017

Tesla rolled out the prototype in 2017. 0 to 60 in 1.9 seconds, 620 miles of range, top speed over 250 mph. $200,000 for the base car, $250,000 for the Founders Series. Deliveries were supposed to start in 2020.
Those are still the only official numbers. Reservations are still $50,000 each.
Source: Reuters: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tesla-set-unveil-next-generation-100401819.html
Source: Teslarati: https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-roadster-livestream-waco-texas/

[IMAGE: /workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-15/roadster2-jurvetson.jpg]
Caption: The next-gen Roadster prototype at Tesla HQ, April 2019. Photo: Steve Jurvetson, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons.

### Then he added rockets

June 2018, Elon on X: "SpaceX option package for new Tesla Roadster will include ~10 small rocket thrusters arranged seamlessly around car... Maybe they will even allow a Tesla to fly."
Source: https://x.com/elonmusk/status/1005577738332172289

Then in 2024 he said the redesigned car does 0 to 60 in under a second, "and that is least interesting part," and called it a Tesla and SpaceX collab.
Source: Reuters: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tesla-set-unveil-next-generation-100401819.html

Teslarati reports the SpaceX package is code named A71. Reported, not confirmed by Tesla.
Source: https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-roadster-livestream-waco-texas/

[IMAGE: /workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-15/f9-cold-gas-thrusters.jpg]
Caption: Cold gas thrusters on a Falcon 9 first stage as it lands at LZ-1, Feb 2017. This is the kind of hardware he's talking about. SpaceX, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.

### A drag race doesn't care about wind

Tesla pushed the original Oct 1 date for severe weather and said the demo can only be held outdoors. Elon said the problem was high winds. For that first date the FAA closed the airspace over the McGregor site up to 10,000 feet.
You don't close airspace for a quarter mile run. Just saying.
Source: Teslarati: https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-roadster-livestream-waco-texas/
Source: Tesla on X (new time): https://x.com/Tesla/status/2110165104713183485

And McGregor is the place SpaceX already made a spacecraft hover on thrusters. They did it with the DragonFly test article in 2015.

[IMAGE: /workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-15/dragonfly-hover-mcgregor.jpg]
Caption: SpaceX's DragonFly test article firing its eight SuperDraco engines in a tethered hover test, Nov 2015, at SpaceX's McGregor, Texas facility. SpaceX, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.

### The case that none of this matters

Elon himself called the Roadster a "small potato situation" in 2023 that won't "move the needle in a major way financially." Morningstar expects about 2,000 a year. Tesla sold 1.6 million cars last year. This year Tesla even stopped selling the Model S and X to make room for Optimus.
So why a rocket site and a live stream? Halo car. Morningstar's Seth Goldstein: "The Roadster will be Tesla's new halo car."
Source: Reuters: https://finance.yahoo.com/news/tesla-set-unveil-next-generation-100401819.html

And the timing is wild. Porsche unveils its Mission S mid-engine supercar concept in New York at 4:30pm ET today, about four hours before Tesla. According to Autocar it's combustion only, and the teaser shows an 8,000 rpm tach. Old school vs rocket school, same night.
Source: Paul Tan: https://paultan.org/2026/10/15/porsche-mission-s-teased-under-wraps-mid-engined-supercar-above-the-911-gets-a-roof-scoop/
Source: Road & Track: https://www.roadandtrack.com/news/a74111038/porsche-mission-s-concept-everything-we-know-so-far/

[IMAGE: /workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-15/starman.jpg]
Caption: The last time Elon put a Roadster near a rocket. Starman in his original Roadster after the Falcon Heavy launch, Feb 2018. SpaceX, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons.

### Where I land

If that car leaves the ground tonight, nobody remembers the six year delay. That's the whole bet.
But the flex isn't the hover. It's a production date. Teslarati says Giga Texas in 2027 or 2028, and Tesla says the event will include pricing, specs and production targets. Q3 earnings are Oct 21, six days later, so we'll find out fast if those numbers hold.
If it's just a really fast launch, it's a $200k car six years late with a cool trailer.
Source: https://www.teslarati.com/tesla-roadster-livestream-waco-texas/

Does it fly tonight or not?
