## BODY

Jay Stratton ran the Pentagon's UAP Task Force. This week he went on Fox and said he has seen non-human craft and non-human beings with his own eyes. I'm open to it. I also want one file.

### What he actually said

Bret Baier asked him straight up if the government has non-human craft. "Yes, it does." Has he seen one? "I have seen non-human beings and non-human craft in my own eyes." He said both the government and defense contractors have them, and that there's "an ongoing program to capture and reverse engineer this technology."
Source: Fox News, Special Report: https://www.foxnews.com/video/6406725522112

The timing wasn't random. His memoir, Out of the Shadows, went on sale Oct 13.
Source: HarperCollins: https://www.harpercollins.com/products/out-of-the-shadows-jay-stratton

Caption: Still from the Navy "Gimbal" video, one of three the Pentagon officially released in 2020. U.S. Navy, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

### The resume is real. Don't pretend it isn't

Even Avi Loeb, the Harvard astrophysicist pushing back hardest this week, says Stratton has "high professional credibility." 32 years on the battlefield and in defense intel, director of the UAP Task Force, a civilian rank equal to a two-star general.
Source: Avi Loeb on X: https://x.com/ProfAviLoeb/status/2110124461118648826

Per Fox's own summary, he talked about military encounters with black triangle craft, and about nuclear submarines being used as bait to track these things. He also told Baier he's briefed Congress plenty of times, starting with Senator Harry Reid in 2007. Fair point. He didn't only go to TV.
Source: https://www.foxnews.com/video/6406725522112

[IMAGE: /workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-14/uss-texas-north-pole.jpg]
Caption: The attack submarine USS Texas surfaced near the North Pole, 2009. U.S. Navy photo, public domain, via Wikimedia Commons.

### The Pentagon says the exact opposite

In 2024 the Pentagon's own UFO office, AARO, went back through every government UAP effort since 1945. Its words: "AARO found no empirical evidence for claims that the USG and private companies have been reverse-engineering extraterrestrial technology."
Source: AARO Historical Record Report Vol. I (March 2024): https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/dod/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Volume_1_2024.pdf

Baier put that to him on air. Stratton's answer: "Somebody's wrong."
He's right about that much. Both can't be true.

And the famous Navy videos? The Pentagon released them in 2020 and still calls what's in them "unidentified." Unidentified isn't alien. It's unidentified.
Source: DoD statement, April 27, 2020: https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/

### Fox before the scientists

Loeb says he invited Stratton to brief the UAP Science Advisory Council after they met. "Jay did not respond to my invitation." Then he asked the question everyone's thinking: why is Fox a higher priority than a room full of scientists?
Source: Avi Loeb on X: https://x.com/ProfAviLoeb/status/2110083332511760435

### Where I land

A serious guy's testimony is still testimony. If these craft exist, there is sensor data. A photo. A piece of material. One release ends this argument either way, and it doesn't need to reveal how anything works.
I'm not calling him a liar. I'm not calling this disclosure either.
What would you need to see before you believe it?
