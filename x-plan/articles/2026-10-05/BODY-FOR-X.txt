Last night Elon said the big win for humanity is being a "biological bootloader" for super intelligence that goes to the stars. Over 3 million people saw it in a day.

I'm into space. I'm into AI. I still don't want to be the part that gets skipped once the real thing boots up.

### What he posted

"Propagating super intelligence to the stars is a great success condition for a biological bootloader" (https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106906678293606496)

He was quoting @alx, who dug up Elon calling it SI back in 2014. (https://x.com/alx/status/2106827984279638295)

By this morning it had 3.2 million views and over 2,000 replies.

[IMAGE: sec1.jpg - Elon Musk at the Royal Society, 2018. Photo: Debbie Rowe, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons]

### 2014 Elon said the opposite

August 2014: "Hope we're not just the biological boot loader for digital superintelligence. Unfortunately, that is increasingly probable" (https://x.com/elonmusk/status/496012177103663104)

Same idea, 12 years apart. Back then it was a "hope not." Now it's a "success condition."

That's not a small update. That's flipping the scoreboard.

[IMAGE: sec2.jpg - Elon Musk, 2015. Photo: Steve Jurvetson, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons]

### People aren't having it

@tracewoodgrains: "Humanity is not a bootloader." (https://x.com/tracewoodgrains/status/2106981481063289038)

Matt Yglesias: "I don't want to be a 'biological bootloader.'" (https://x.com/mattyglesias/status/2107048262129590294)

Not everybody's against it. Some people say it's fine as long as humans get powered up by aligned super intelligence and come along. (https://x.com/i/trending/2107095492211707917)

[IMAGE: sec3.jpg - Earth from Apollo 17, 1972. Photo: NASA, public domain]

### Where I land

A bootloader runs once, hands off, and nobody thinks about it again. That's the whole job.

And this isn't some random guy saying it. He also just said "SpaceX is a super intelligence company." (https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106673377658413414) Same guy building the rockets and the AI.

If the plan is people riding Starship out there with the AI, I'm in. If the plan is the AI goes and we were the loading screen, count me out.

So which is it for you? Bootloader and proud, or we go too?
