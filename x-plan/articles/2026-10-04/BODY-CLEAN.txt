Musk just said SpaceXAI is becoming SpaceXSI. Second rename in about three months. Still no date.

I'm not mad about SI as a word. I'm mad that we keep getting name swaps and calling it a finished story.

### What he actually said

Overnight on X he posted "No more AI," then "SI," then "It's better." (https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106665679361618173)

Then: "SpaceX is a super intelligence company." (https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106673377658413414)

Someone asked if it could be SpaceXSI. He replied: "Yes, we will make that change." (https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106672603536712025) (https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/04/elon-musk-spacexai-spacexsi-rename/)

That's the whole announcement so far. Three posts. One yes.


### We've done this rename already

July 6, 2026: xAI became SpaceXAI. New logo. Handle flipped to @SpaceXAI. (https://www.businessinsider.com/xai-rebrand-spacexai-new-logo-x-handle-spacex-2026-7) (https://www.engadget.com/2209300/xai-now-officially-spacexai/)

Ownership already moved earlier when SpaceX took xAI. July was branding. Now we're renaming the branding again before the paint is dry. (https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/04/elon-musk-spacexai-spacexsi-rename/)

SpaceXAI as a public name lasted under three months. That's not a legacy. That's a sticky note.


### What's missing

No effective date. No legal-name filing called out in the coverage. No new logo drop. @SpaceXAI still the live handle as of this morning's writeups. No company blog post. (https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/04/elon-musk-spacexai-spacexsi-rename/)

A yes on X is a signal. It is not a rollout.

If the product, the API, the billing entity, and the app icon stay the same, we renamed a vibe.


### Closing

I like the ambition. Super intelligence as the goal is fine. Shipping another name before the last one settles is how you teach people to ignore your branding.

Show me the date, the logo, and the handle flip. Until then it's a teaser with good engagement.

Question: do you count a rename as real when there's no date and the old handle is still up, or is that just another Sunday quote?
