# SpaceXAI lasted three months. Now it's SpaceXSI.

**Status:** PUBLISHED
**Date:** 2026-10-04
**Banner:** /workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-04/banner.png
**Voice:** @P7RIM3 - short, raw, first-person, takes a side

---

## Article body

Musk just said SpaceXAI is becoming SpaceXSI. Second rename in about three months. Still no date.

I'm not mad about SI as a word. I'm mad that we keep getting name swaps and calling it a finished story.

### What he actually said

Overnight on X he posted "No more AI," then "SI," then "It's better." ([Elon Musk](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106665679361618173))

Then: "SpaceX is a super intelligence company." ([Elon Musk](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106673377658413414))

Someone asked if it could be SpaceXSI. He replied: "Yes, we will make that change." ([Elon Musk](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2106672603536712025), [TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/04/elon-musk-spacexai-spacexsi-rename/))

That's the whole announcement so far. Three posts. One yes.

![What he actually said](/workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-04/sec1.png)
*Photo: SpaceX Starbase launch facility. Credit: Wikimedia Commons (Steve Jurvetson / Flickr, CC).*

### We've done this rename already

July 6, 2026: xAI became SpaceXAI. New logo. Handle flipped to @SpaceXAI. ([Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/xai-rebrand-spacexai-new-logo-x-handle-spacex-2026-7), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2209300/xai-now-officially-spacexai/))

Ownership already moved earlier when SpaceX took xAI. July was branding. Now we're renaming the branding again before the paint is dry. ([TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/04/elon-musk-spacexai-spacexsi-rename/))

SpaceXAI as a public name lasted under three months. That's not a legacy. That's a sticky note.

![We've done this rename already](/workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-04/sec2.png)
*Photo: Starbase integration tower. Credit: Wikimedia Commons (Steve Jurvetson / Flickr, CC).*

### What's missing

No effective date. No legal-name filing called out in the coverage. No new logo drop. @SpaceXAI still the live handle as of this morning's writeups. No company blog post. ([TeslaNorth](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/04/elon-musk-spacexai-spacexsi-rename/))

A yes on X is a signal. It is not a rollout.

If the product, the API, the billing entity, and the app icon stay the same, we renamed a vibe.

![What's missing](/workspace/x-plan/articles/2026-10-04/sec3.png)
*Photo: SpaceX Starship during IFT-5. Credit: Wikimedia Commons (Steve Jurvetson, CC BY).*

### Closing

I like the ambition. Super intelligence as the goal is fine. Shipping another name before the last one settles is how you teach people to ignore your branding.

Show me the date, the logo, and the handle flip. Until then it's a teaser with good engagement.

Question: do you count a rename as real when there's no date and the old handle is still up, or is that just another Sunday quote?
