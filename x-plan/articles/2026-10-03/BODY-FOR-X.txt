An orb crossed live LA traffic TV around Sept. 22. The clip went viral. Then the story just stopped.

I'm not here to sell you aliens. I'm here because a major station put a dark sphere on air and nobody finished the homework.

### What the clip shows

FOX 11's traffic helicopter filmed a dark spherical object during a freeway report. The camera stayed on it for several seconds. By about Sept. 22 the clip was circulating hard. ([UFO Disclosure](https://ufodisclosure.org/los-angeles-orb-live-tv-uap/), [IBTimes UK Sept 25](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/fox-11-helicopter-films-orb-over-los-angeles-freeway-1821949))

No visible wings. No engines you can point at. Straight-line path gets argued in the replies. That's the clip. That's all we actually have on video.

### Size guesses aren't data

People keep calling it basketball-size. That is not a measurement.

Distance is unknown. Altitude is unknown. Speed is unknown. Exact freeway ID never locked. When you don't know range, every size claim is vibes. ([UFO Disclosure](https://ufodisclosure.org/los-angeles-orb-live-tv-uap/), [IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/mysterious-object-los-angeles-freeway-balloon-drone-debate-1822653), [SGIB](https://www.sgibinc.com/en/losangeles-ufo-fox11/))

Footage alone is not enough to confirm size, distance, or identity. Balloon, drone, aircraft, optical effect. All still possible on the video we have. ([IBTimes UK](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/mysterious-object-los-angeles-freeway-balloon-drone-debate-1822653))

### Balloon still on the table

I'm open to weird sky stuff. I'm not open to skipping the boring options.

AARO's public stats (reports 1996 through Oct. 2024): about 31% of UAP reports get described as orb, round, or sphere. That's second only to lights at 46%. Among cases they resolved: roughly 75% balloons, 16% UAS/drones, 6% birds. ([SGIB](https://www.sgibinc.com/en/losangeles-ufo-fox11/))

There is no public AARO link tying this FOX 11 clip to a closed case. Balloon stays plausible when wind data exists on similar orbs. Drone stays live too. Uncatalogued is not a species. ([UFO Disclosure](https://ufodisclosure.org/los-angeles-orb-live-tv-uap/), [SGIB](https://www.sgibinc.com/en/losangeles-ufo-fox11/))

Check trackers first. Publicly available tracking was not published with the clip. That gap is on the coverage, not on your feed.

[IMAGE: sec3.png Balloon still on the table - Graphic: @P7RIM3. AARO resolved-orb pattern from public summary.]

### The follow-up that never came

Here's my actual beef.

No second camera angle went public. No published radar. No known FOX 11 technical analysis. No formal statement from aviation authorities in the coverage that followed. No public ID. ([UFO Disclosure](https://ufodisclosure.org/los-angeles-orb-live-tv-uap/), [IBTimes UK Sept 25](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/fox-11-helicopter-films-orb-over-los-angeles-freeway-1821949))

Viral for a few days. Then silence. That's the story. Not "ET over the 405." Journalism stopped when the clicks moved on.

PURSUE releases exist on the public portal if you want the broader paper trail. I'm not claiming this LA clip is in them. ([war.gov/UFO](https://www.war.gov/UFO/))

### Closing

Live TV put an orb on screen over LA traffic. Fine. Phenomenon can be real. Balloon and drone still belong in the room.

What I won't shrug off is the drop: no second angle, no radar drop, no official ID, story gone. If a station films it live, finish the check or say you couldn't.

Question: next time a traffic helo locks an orb on live TV, do you want a second camera and a radar pull, or another week of vibes and then nothing?
