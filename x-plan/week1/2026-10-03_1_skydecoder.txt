How to identify a thing in the sky without sounding unserious:

1. Time, direction, angular speed, color, whether it blinks.
2. FlightRadar24 for planes.
3. Heavens-Above / Find Starlink for satellites.
4. If it matches a catalog, you are done.
5. If it does not match, write “uncatalogued,” not “craft.”

Uncatalogued is an interesting sentence.
Aliens is a conclusion you have not earned.
